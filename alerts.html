What powers this forecast?

GloFAS uses a hydrological model driven by weather forecasts. ExploreGuard retrieves its published output through Open-Meteo; we do not train or run this global model in your browser.

What does the number mean?

The raw 0–1 value is NASA’s rainfall-triggered landslide model output for an approximately 1 km grid cell. It is not a validated probability for your home, road or hillside. We do not convert it into a safety category. Check the official map’s date and legend and follow local warnings.

Use official local warnings for safety decisions. Forecasts can be wrong or unavailable. Sources: Open-Meteo, Copernicus GloFAS and NASA LHASA.

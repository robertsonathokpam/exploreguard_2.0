Hackathon Innovation Highlights

Engineered for real-world humanitarian impact

A working prototype for provider data, photo analysis and fictional incident reports, with clear limits on what each output means.

Weather & terrain observations
Retrieves Open-Meteo modelled conditions and elevation for a selected location. No unsupported disaster probabilities or evacuation instructions.
Photo person detection
Runs RT-DETR or COCO-SSD on uploaded photos and webcam frames in your browser. Counts need evaluation; no venue density or safe route is inferred.
AI Golden-Hour Incident reports
Records fictional reports with unknown observations preserved. Optional Gemini draft summaries support human review; no clinical priority or real dispatch is provided.
Consent-Driven GPS Tracker
Privacy-first hardware coordinate telemetry with zero background logging. Instant emergency link sharing and cloud coordinate clearance upon toggle deactivation.
Localized Emergency Directory
Granular district-level database of police, tertiary trauma centers, fire commands, and disaster rescue teams with one-tap dialing.
Unified Transit & Permit Console
Centralized transit permit bookings and active boarding passes synchronized via Cloud Firestore real-time NoSQL storage.

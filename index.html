Hackathon Innovation Highlights

Engineered for real-world humanitarian impact

Combining multi-sensor meteorological feeds, geomorphological terrain modeling, pedestrian density dynamics, and clinical triage protocols.

AI Landslide & Disaster Predictor
Ingests live OpenWeather telemetry + Manipur highway terrain weights (NH-2/NH-37 & flood basins) to calculate real-time probability scores and evacuation advisories.
Kumbh Mela Crowd Telemetry
Sector-by-sector crowd density monitoring (persons/m²), automated chokepoint bottleneck triggers, and dynamic decongestion rerouting protocols.
AI Golden-Hour Medical Triage
Clinical START/Manchester triage for stampede trauma, heatstroke, and landslide casualties. Renders offline first-aid steps and logs GPS SOS dispatches to Firestore.
Consent-Driven GPS Tracker
Privacy-first hardware coordinate telemetry with zero background logging. Instant emergency link sharing and cloud coordinate clearance upon toggle deactivation.
Localized Emergency Directory
Granular district-level database of police, tertiary trauma centers, fire commands, and disaster rescue teams with one-tap dialing.
Unified Transit & Permit Console
Centralized transit permit bookings and active boarding passes synchronized via Cloud Firestore real-time NoSQL storage.
